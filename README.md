# Abenly 

Abenly is a modern, fast, and privacy-focused Android application designed to track both your vehicle maintenance history and recurring subscriptions in one place.

## ✨ Key Features
- 🛠 **Vehicle Maintenance Tracking:** Log service records, oil changes, vehicle inspections, and routine care with automatic calculation of upcoming due dates.
- 💳 **Subscription Management:** Keep track of recurring expenses (monthly, yearly, or custom frequencies) and view your total estimated monthly cost at a glance.
- 📅 **Due Date Planning:** Easily see when tasks were last performed and anticipate your next scheduled maintenance or payments.
- 🔒 **100% Offline & Private:** No account required, no network access needed—all your data stays strictly on your device.
- 🎨 **Modern Interface:** Built entirely with Jetpack Compose and Material 3 design guidelines.

## 🛠 Tech Stack
- **Language:** Kotlin
- **UI:** Jetpack Compose, Material 3
- **Storage:** DataStore / Preferences
- **Architecture:** Android Architecture Components
